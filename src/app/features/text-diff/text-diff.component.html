info How it works

  1. Enter or paste your original text in the left field
  2. Enter or paste your modified text in the right field
  3. Choose a Diff Mode: Smart normalizes whitespace, Unicode, and quotes before diffing (great for docs & AI text); Strict does an exact comparison. Fine-tune with Ignore Whitespace / Case / Punctuation toggles.
  4. Click Compare — changed lines show as a - old / + new pair with word-level highlights
  5. Switch between Unified and Split views for different perspectives

lock 100% Private: All comparison happens in your browser. No data is sent to any server.
